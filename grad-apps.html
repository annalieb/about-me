Why a PhD?

I am pursuing a PhD for two main reasons:

  1. To gain methodological expertise for researching increasingly complex and impactful sociotechnical systems. Most recently, I have worked on descriptive research projects that answer "what" questions about online information and social media. I am increasingly interested in answering "why" and "how" questions to shed light on underlying mechanisms of sociotechnical systems, especially AI systems. Specifically, in my PhD studies I hope to learn more about causal inference design and AI auditing/evaluation methods.
  2. To prepare for a long-term career in research. Over the years, I've found that I really enjoy doing research 🤓. It seems like a PhD is one of the best ways to learn from a community of other researchers and learn by doing. I am especially interested in pursuing a career in public-facing research, whether in academia, government, or civil society organizations.
See the GRFP Research Plan I submitted in October for an example of how I imagine my research could look in the next few years. (Of course, this plan is a starting point and I'm not wedded to it!)
