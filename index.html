Hello! My name is Anna, and I'm a Computational Social Science Research Analyst on the Data Labs team at Pew Research Center, where I study social media, online information, and digital behavior.

I graduated summa cum laude from Wellesley College with a BA in Data Science (Computer Science concentration) and Political Science, completing an honors thesis on political issue framing in online news coverage.

I'm drawn to the many ways people interact with technology — especially at the intersection of computational social science, human-computer interaction, and technology policy — and I try to bring methods like NLP, machine learning, and web data collection to bear on questions of wellbeing and justice in tech.

You can find the full story, including my publications, research experience, and skills, on my CV.
