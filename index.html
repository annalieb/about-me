Hello! My name is Anna, and I'm a Research Analyst at Pew Research Center. In my role on the Data Labs team, I work on computational social science projects related to social media, online information, and digital behavior. Before Pew, I graduated from Wellesley College with a BA in Data Science (CS concentration) and Political Science.

My research interests include online information dynamics (especially related to news & politics) and how people interact with digital platforms such as social media, search engines, and large language models. My work often uses methods in NLP, machine learning, and web data collection. As an undergrad, I conducted research on human-AI interaction and political issue framing in online news media. More information is available on my CV.
